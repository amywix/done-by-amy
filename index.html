FAQ

Automation without the tech headache.

Do I need to replace my current software?

No. The goal is usually to make better use of the systems you already pay for and connect the gaps between them.

Can you automate social media and messages?

Yes. We can design workflows around social enquiries, customer messages, lead capture, notifications and follow-up.

Can you connect multiple systems?

Yes. We can design workflows that move information between supported forms, email, spreadsheets, CRMs, calendars, workforce systems, accounting platforms and messaging tools.

Will automation replace my staff?

The focus is on removing repetitive admin so your team can spend more time on customers, sales and work that actually needs a human.

What if I don't know what to automate?

That's exactly what the automation audit is for. Tell us what your staff repeatedly do and we'll help identify the best opportunities.
